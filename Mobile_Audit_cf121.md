# Phone audit

Tested representative mobile-suit, infantry, tank and carrier sheets, equipment and base menus, roster, portrait main menu, and Firefight challenge selection at three iPhone-sized landscape viewports. Screenshots are in tests/screenshots/mobile-*.png.

Fixed: challenge list viewport reduced to roughly one partial row by heading/footer; undersized Cancel and objective input; small damage-amount controls; cramped squad mode tabs; ship/vehicle row descriptions overlapping; disabled pinch zoom. Challenge controls now meet 44×44px, squad buttons are 64px high, scrolling reaches the fourth squad, and selections/lineup still work.

Remaining: the illustrated sheets scale to fit landscape and contain small rings, AP steppers, weapon rows and footer shortcuts. Pinch zoom is now available. Replacing those layouts with a separate touch interface would be a larger change. Portrait battle sheets intentionally show the existing rotate prompt; portrait main menu remains usable and scrollable. No physical iPhone or Safari verification was available in this audit.
