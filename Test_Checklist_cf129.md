# cf129 quick test

1. Tap Pilot on the main menu. Check the clash transition and hangar window battle scene.
2. Try Female/Male, all four faces, hair styles and uniforms. Change hair, eyebrow and eye colours separately; check the skin and eye whites stay unchanged.
3. Try uniform colours, then Original. Check the fit around the neck and shoulders.
4. Enter a name, callsign and background under Identity. Save pilot, refresh and reopen Pilot.
5. Change an option, tap Menu, and test both Keep editing and Discard changes.
6. Check a small landscape phone and portrait orientation: controls remain reachable and the portrait keeps its proportions.
7. After the app has finished caching, go offline and reopen Pilot. Try an appearance you had not used before.
8. Use the bottom Play Offline / Continue Offline entry. Check that your existing battle is still present, and Play Online still opens the lobby.

This milestone saves one editable pilot per browser/device. Campaign GP, unit traits and backup/export remain future milestones.
