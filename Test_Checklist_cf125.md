# cf125 — Quick playtest

Confirm all devices show **cf125**. Earlier cf124 functionality remains included.

1. **Infantry loading:** confirm a team with squads and vehicles. Tap a squad: it should highlight without moving. Tap a highlighted vehicle, try a full vehicle, then move a squad to On the board. Back discards the plan; Confirm & deploy saves it.
2. **Ship repair bay:** select a boarded unit, then choose one of three highlighted slots. Try deselecting, Cancel selection, replacing an occupied slot and clearing it. Check the portrait, current HP and next-turn forecast. Dock turn 1 → aboard/assign turn 2 → repair turn 3.
3. **Shields and Inventory:** open Equip → Inventory, choose a stored shield and tap an R/L shield HP bubble. Cancel a swap once, then confirm. Only the confirmed equip spends 1 AP, the shield keeps its HP, and the hand weapon stays unchanged. Stow uses the same bubble; an empty slot can be equipped again.
4. **Base owner and entry:** as leader, choose an owner from the player list. As that owner, tap Slot 1, cancel once, then confirm base territory. Check the green slot, portrait and shared roster symbol/dimming. A second damaged entrant warns before replacing the current unit.
5. **Base repairs:** enter on your turn 1 and advance through the enemy turn. At the start of your turn 2, expect up to +2 HP per intact location and +5 per eligible shield, capped at maximum. Zero-HP limbs stay at zero. Leave base and check the symbol clears and healing stops.
6. **Damage and death:** repeatedly apply damage on a suit, ship and soldier sheet. Tables, artwork and buttons should stay still. Destroy a suit: the overlay must span the sheet and cover the L/R labels, “?” and all bottom buttons. Correct the fatal HP to remove it.
7. **Phone layout:** check both infantry groups and all three top tabs in landscape, then select squads in Challenge. Scroll the loading/repair lists as needed; portraits, selection highlights and Confirm/Close controls should remain usable.

If something differs, record the unit, action, build, owner, turn number and online/offline mode.
