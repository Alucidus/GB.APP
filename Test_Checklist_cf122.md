# cf122 — Quick playtest

Use a fresh test battle and confirm the menu says cf122. For shared pickups, use two devices in the same online room.

1. **Phone layout:** rotate to landscape. Check readable lists, AP buttons, compact L/R labels, and that the Gundam keeps its proportions. On infantry and ships, try Move, Done and ?; none should cover another control. Check that the footer backgrounds follow their buttons.
2. **Equip:** equip one Beam Saber from a ×2 set; expect ×1 in storage. Equip the second; expect ×0 and melee Advantage in the purple status box. Use a ranged weapon alongside melee; check the −3 ranged modifier. Equipping onto an occupied arm should open the in-game swap warning; Cancel keeps everything unchanged.
3. **Drop and recover:** lose an arm holding a weapon/shield. Open Pick Up; your own items should appear first. Repairing the arm alone must not return the item. Cancel the pickup confirmation, then confirm a pickup within 10cm; expect exactly 1 AP spent.
4. **Enemy gear:** destroy an enemy carrying a weapon. Pick it up from another unit. An empty compatible hand should equip it; otherwise find it under Equip > More. Its normal five-row weapon list must remain unchanged. The drop should disappear on both devices.
5. **Captured weapon details:** tap the copper/yellow arm label. Check damage, range, AP and remaining charges/cooldown. Fire from that panel and confirm the correct AP/resource change. Reload: the captured item and remaining resources should persist.
6. **Captured shield:** pick up a damaged physical shield and confirm its HP is unchanged. Adjust its HP from the matching arm panel. A destroyed shield cannot be picked up; a captured physical shield should not gain beam-shield regeneration.
7. **Ground base:** use BASE and confirm territory. Enter with damaged, intact locations; advance to the next own turn. Expect +2 location HP and +5 eligible shield HP. Destroyed limbs must remain destroyed.
8. **Carrier repair:** dock during your turn 1. It completes on your turn 2; the first repair is your turn 3. Expect +4 intact-location HP and +12 eligible physical shield HP. Check the three service slots and queue against the ship's separate hangar capacity.
9. **Firefight selection:** on a phone, select squads further down both lists, change the pairing, and check that Cancel/Challenge remain reachable.

If something fails, note the unit, action, phone orientation, online/offline mode and turn number. A screenshot of the problem is useful.
