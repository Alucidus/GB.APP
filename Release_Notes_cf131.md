# cf131 — Pilot Studio and fitted hairstyles

The character creator now uses the user's hand-fitted 1254 × 1500 PSD artwork. All fitted layer coordinates and sizes are preserved, including male dreadlocks. The new hairstyles use the cleaned edge artwork.

- 12 male hairstyles and 9 female hairstyles, including the previous three for each body.
- Five facial-hair overlays plus clean shaven, with independent colour selection.
- Facial hair draws below the hairstyle, above the face and uniform.
- Illustrated hairstyle, face and facial-hair thumbnails; selected cards show a clear highlight and checkmark.
- Refined hangar studio with readable panels, framed portrait and responsive phone layouts.
- Existing pilot records and battle saves remain compatible. New artwork and thumbnails are cached for offline use.

Validation: 68 layout/save/menu checks across five desktop/phone viewports; 126 fitted creator checks across all faces, hairstyles and beards; 256 hair/uniform asset checks; 33 iris checks; 9 service-worker/offline integration checks. Chrome was tested with touch emulation; physical iPhone Safari was not tested.

No GP, rank mechanics or full unit-record functionality was added in this release.
