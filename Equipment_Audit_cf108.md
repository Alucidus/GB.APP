# Equipment audit — cf108

All 50 mobile-suit entries inspected. Equipment enforcement covers 48; Rising Freedom and Infinite Justice remain excluded for rework. Phenex uses hand equipment for its sabers; its DE system is unchanged. Approved cf108 profiles are recorded in Release_Notes_cf108.md and the rulebook. Banshee AA-DE Melee Mode has been removed.

## Still undefined

These custom melee roll bonuses remain unspecified. Existing damage and charge ranges are preserved; the app does not invent a bonus.
