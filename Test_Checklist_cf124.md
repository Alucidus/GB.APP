# cf124 — Quick test checklist

Confirm the menu shows **cf124** on every device.

1. **Shield stow:** press Stow, then tap an equipped shield HP bubble. Its HP and the hand weapon should stay unchanged; no AP is spent.
2. **Two shields:** collect two damaged shields and equip one on each arm. R/L bubbles must show their separate remaining HP. Swap one through Equip, cancel once, then confirm; only the confirmed equip spends 1 AP. Add a Phenex loan and check its separate bubble.
3. **Pickup list:** check the unit portraits, source names and your lost items appearing first. Claim an item online and confirm it disappears on the other device.
4. **Carrier bay:** select one of the three numbered slots, then a boarded unit. Check portrait, current HP and next repair amounts. A docking unit must be unavailable until fully aboard. Dock turn 1, board/assign turn 2, heal turn 3. Try clearing/replacing a slot; the displaced unit stays aboard.
5. **Base:** open the menu without entering—no roster dimming. Confirm entry; check the base symbol and dimmed row on your roster, the enemy roster and the sheet picker. Leave and confirm both disappear.
6. **Base repair slot:** check the one-slot layout and units-in-base list. Choose another waiting unit for slot 1. Expect the displayed Tier 0 amounts (+2 per intact location / +5 per eligible shield, capped at maximum) on its next eligible repair. Carrier rates remain +4/+12. Destroyed limbs stay at zero.
7. **Reload and phone layout:** reload after equipment/slot changes and check they persist. On phone landscape, confirm both shield bubbles, the borrowed shield, all three repair slots, roster choices and Close/Confirm buttons remain usable by scrolling where necessary.
8. **Infantry:** switch among the three slim, full-width tabs. Check soldier artwork and both groups of four. Try Overmap Move/Undo, then Quick Resolve → Challenge: choose squads on both sides, scroll to the last squad and change a matchup. The squad list and Cancel/Challenge buttons should remain usable in a short window.

If something fails, record the unit, action, build, online/offline mode and turn number, plus a screenshot if possible.
