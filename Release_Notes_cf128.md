# cf128 - Compact damage toolbar on small devices

Small screens show damage amounts 1, 2, 3, 4, custom #, and nuclear blast in one row. Larger screens retain all existing presets. Custom values above 4 remain visible in the custom control, and resizing preserves the selected amount. Touch controls remain reachable without adding a second toolbar row that takes space from the sheet.

Validation: 252 browser checks across six viewport sizes and mobile suit, ship and infantry sheets, including preset taps, custom input, nuclear-dialog cancellation and breakpoint resizing. Infantry layout checks passed at four small viewport sizes. Chrome touch emulation was used; physical Safari was not tested.

Includes all cf127 changes. Build, asset URLs and offline cache: cf128. No deployment.
