# cf131 quick review

1. Open Pilot from the menu. Check the hangar portrait and hairstyle tiles in portrait and landscape.
2. Browse every hairstyle and all four faces for both bodies. Check your fitted dreads, undercuts and long styles.
3. On a male pilot, select Facial hair. Try each option and a different colour. Long hair should cover the beard where they overlap.
4. Change hair, eyebrows, eye and uniform colours. Check that the eyes retain clean pupils and highlights.
5. Save a named pilot, reload, and confirm the chosen hair and facial hair return.
6. After the app has loaded online, reload offline and select a hairstyle or beard you have not opened before.
7. Check that Play/Continue Offline still opens your existing battle normally.
