# cf127 — Quick check

Confirm the app shows **cf127**.

1. Tap each mobile-suit HP bubble. Its minus and plus buttons should sit immediately beside it. Try both adjustments, resize/rotate and check they stay attached. Shield controls should remain together too.
2. Open a damaged unit's personal base. Slot 1 should initially be inactive. Tap the unit's health card: it highlights and enables the slot, without entering the base.
3. Cancel selection once, then reselect the unit and tap Slot 1. Confirm territory. Check that the unit has one portrait/card with its health and forecast, and the slot shows its occupant.
4. With another damaged unit waiting in base, select its card and then the slot. Cancel/confirm the replacement warning. Healing timing and Leave base should work as before.

All previous cf126/cf125 functions remain included.
